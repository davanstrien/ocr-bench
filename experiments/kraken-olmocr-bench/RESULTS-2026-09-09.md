# Full Kraken run — 9 September 2026

Kraken's bundled BLLA segmentation and PP-OCRv6 medium recognition scored
**36.8% overall** on all **1,403 olmOCR-bench pages**, with a 95% bootstrap
confidence interval of **35.9–37.7%**. All pages completed; the official scorer
evaluated **8,413 tests** with zero unresolved producer or candidate errors.

## Configuration and validation

The recognizer has approximately 15.92M parameters; the segmenter is additional.
Pages were rendered to RGB at 300 DPI and processed in CUDA fp32, batch size 32,
with four Torch CPU threads and four A10G-small shards. Output was raw
newline-joined text without dehyphenation, header suppression, table reconstruction
or LaTeX generation. The official scorer ran on CPU-upgrade.

All inference, runtime checks and scoring ran on Hugging Face Jobs. The 21-page
smoke verified identical ordered geometry and text for stock/staged inference
and completed-page resumption without model calls. Staging did not establish a
meaningful speedup.

## Runtime and cost

- Segmentation: **24,390.1s**, or **85.5%** of aggregate measured inference time.
- Recognition: **4,139.8s**. Total aggregate inference: **7.925 hours**.
- Full inference including recovery: **2h 26m 25s** elapsed; through scoring
  completion: **2h 53m 28s**.
- **$9.56** estimated list-rate compute, or **$9.65** with each Job rounded up to
  a minute, including all 15 setup/smoke/full/recovery/scoring attempts. All stopped.
  Costs are before credits and are not an invoice.

Three very large old scans needed page-specific Pillow size allowances and
resumed inference after the original attempt completed 1,400 pages. Their
resolution and model settings stayed unchanged. The first full scorer attempt
failed on a transient Hub HTTP 502; bounded download retries allowed scoring to
complete. Both source revisions and every attempt remain accounted for.

## Interpretation and artifacts

These are pipeline test pass rates, not CER or isolated reading-order accuracy.
Headers/footers mainly test omission, and order tests require matching text
anchors. In multiple columns, 356 of 372 failures lacked an anchor, preventing
the test from assessing order. The model's training card does not quantify the
historical share of its training data; this run does not isolate a domain-transfer
effect.
